The approach

Quiet in the room. Loud in the gallery.

Good event photography is invisible while it happens and impossible to ignore afterwards.

Jeremy Allen of The Full Time Hobby has spent years shooting for outdoor and action brands like Arc'teryx, Red Bull and Teton Gravity Research. Fast-moving, low-light, can't-ask-for-a-retake work is the job. Conference stages and dim ballrooms play to the same strengths.

We plan the run-of-show with you, know which faces matter, and make sure every speaker, sponsor and award winner is covered.

FAQ

Questions planners ask.

Do you travel outside Whistler?

Yes. We are based in the Sea to Sky corridor and cover Whistler, Squamish, Pemberton and Vancouver events regularly.

How quickly do we get photos?

Turnaround is agreed before the event. Same-day selects for social media and press can be built into the schedule, with the full edited gallery delivered afterwards.

Can you run a headshot station?

Yes. A portable lit setup can run alongside your event so attendees, speakers and staff leave with a professional headshot.

How are photos delivered?

In a private online gallery your team can browse, download and share, sized for both web and print.

Can sponsors use the photos?

Usage is agreed up front. Most packages include use for your own marketing, and sponsor usage can be added.

Do you also shoot video?

Yes. The Full Time Hobby also produces event recap and brand video. Ask about combining photo and video coverage.
